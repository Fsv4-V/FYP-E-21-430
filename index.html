Project Abstract

Project Overview

Sensing Principle

Potentiometric pH

PANI WE + Ag/AgCl RE cell voltage measured as Vcell = K − 59 mV·pH. Direct Nernstian response, no enzyme needed.

Fabrication Method

Hand-Fabricated FPCB

Screen-printing on PET transparency film. PANI synthesised via oxidative chemical polymerisation. Full protocol in fabrication guide.

Target Application

Diabetic Wound Monitoring

Continuous pH, temperature, and moisture monitoring. BLE 5.0 wireless data to smartphone. Battery life target ≥6 months (CR2032).

Clinical Motivation

The Problem We Are Solving

Diabetic chronic wounds represent a global healthcare crisis that disproportionately affects patients in low-to-middle income countries where affordable continuous monitoring technology is unavailable.

Clinical Wound pH Reference

The sensor must reliably distinguish pH 5.5 (healthy) from pH 8.5 (infected) — a 3-unit range. With PANI's 54–69 mV/pH response, this corresponds to a 162–207 mV signal swing.

Proposed Solution

The Proposed System

A coin-sized, hand-fabricated FPCB wound sensor patch combined with a 20 mm diameter electronics module — fully wireless, battery-powered, and reproducible in a Sri Lankan university laboratory.

  • 🧪PANI potentiometric pH electrode synthesised in-house (~54–60 mV/pH sensitivity target)
  • ⚡Ag/AgCl solid-state reference electrode screen-printed on PET/PI substrate
  • 🌡️NTC 10kΩ bead thermistor for wound surface temperature (±0.5°C, range 20–45°C)
  • 💧Capacitive/impedance-based exudate moisture sensing (100 Hz – 100 kHz AC excitation)
  • 🔌INA333 instrumentation amplifier — 100 dB CMRR, 25 µV offset, 3.3V single supply
  • 📊ADS1115 16-bit I²C ADC — 7.8 µV resolution at ±256 mV PGA range
  • 📡ESP32-S3 with BLE 5.0 + Wi-Fi — deep sleep 10 µA, 240 MHz dual-core
  • 🔋CR2032 coin cell + XC6206 LDO (1 µA quiescent) — target ≥6 months battery life

Sensor Technology

PANI Chemistry

Why Polyaniline (PANI)?

PANI is the only intrinsically pH-responsive conductive polymer. Its Emeraldine Salt form undergoes reversible acid-base doping: PANI-ES + H⁺ ⇌ PANI-ES·H⁺, producing a Nernstian potential change of ~59 mV per pH unit. Unlike PEDOT:PSS or PPy, PANI requires no additional functionalisation for pH sensing. Aniline monomer costs only LKR 250–400 locally (Glorchem LK, Colombo), making it uniquely suited for this low-cost FYP context.
