Project Abstract

Project Overview

Sensing Principle

Potentiometric pH

PANI working electrode + Ag/AgCl reference measured as Vcell = K − 59 mV·pH. Direct Nernstian response, no enzyme required.

Fabrication

Hand-Fabricated FPCB

Screen-printing on PET film. PANI synthesised via oxidative chemical polymerisation in a university lab.

Application

Diabetic Wound Monitoring

Continuous pH, temperature & moisture monitoring with BLE wireless data and target ≥ 6-month battery life.

Clinical Motivation

The Problem We Are Solving

Chronic diabetic wounds are a global healthcare burden that disproportionately affects patients where affordable continuous monitoring is unavailable.

Clinical Wound pH Reference

The sensor must reliably distinguish pH 5.5 (healthy) from pH 8.5 (infected) — a 3-unit range. With PANI's 54–69 mV/pH response, this is a 162–207 mV signal swing, easily resolved by the ADS1115.

      Technical Design

      System Architecture

      The six-layer signal chain from wound contact to wireless output — selected via the Week 04 coin-module comparison.

      Signal Chain

      Analog Front-End

      INA333 (100 dB CMRR) → 100 Hz low-pass filter → ADS1115 16-bit ADC (7.8 µV/LSB).

      Firmware

      ESP32-S3 Logic

      Read ADS1115 (I²C) → 3-point pH calibration → Nernst temperature correction → BLE notify → deep sleep.

      Power

      Ultra-Low Power

      CR2032 + XC6206 (1 µA Iq). Deep sleep 10 µA; BLE advertises only at the measurement interval.

      Transducer Design

      Sensor Technology

      Four sensing modalities on one 30 × 50 mm PET patch, sharing the same electrode pair for pH and impedance moisture sensing.

      PANI Chemistry

      Why Polyaniline (PANI)?

      PANI is the only intrinsically pH-responsive conductive polymer. Its Emeraldine Salt form undergoes reversible acid-base doping (PANI-ES + H⁺ ⇌ PANI-ES·H⁺), producing a Nernstian potential of ~59 mV per pH unit — no extra functionalisation needed. Aniline monomer costs only LKR 250–400 locally (Glorchem LK, Colombo), making it ideal for this low-cost FYP.
